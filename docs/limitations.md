# Limitations

- Only 165 patients are available.
- The cohort comes from one University Hospital in Portugal.
- The data are historical and are not evidence of current HCC survival rates.
- UCI reports approximately 10.22% overall missing data, with very few complete cases.
- The target is binary one-year survival, not exact time-to-event survival.
- Kaplan-Meier curves, Cox models and hazard ratios are therefore not appropriate from this source alone.
- Repeated cross-validation reduces dependence on one split but cannot replace external validation.
- Model coefficients are associative model parameters, not causal effects.
- The model has not been prospectively validated.
- The model must not be used for clinical prognosis, treatment allocation or individual patient decisions.
- Subgroup outcome proportions are unadjusted and can be confounded.
- The dataset is too small to support strong claims about hospital performance or resource allocation.
- Historical clinical coding and practice patterns may not reflect current oncology care.
